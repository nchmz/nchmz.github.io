Shun Zhang

Undergraduate at Anhui University. Focusing on Robotics, Embodied AI, and Control.

I am currently a junior undergraduate student majoring in Electronics Information Engineering at Anhui University (Expected graduation: June 2027), where I rank in the Top 3% of my cohort with a GPA of 4.31/5.0.

My research interests primarily lie at the intersection of Robotics, Embodied AI, Reinforcement Learning, and Computer Vision. I am passionate about bridging the gap between advanced algorithms and real-world physical systems. Currently, my research focuses on adaptive control for omnidirectional vehicles and deploying lightweight deep learning models (e.g., YOLO) onto edge computing platforms like Huawei Ascend CANN.
